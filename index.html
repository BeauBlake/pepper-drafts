Why choose Pepper

More than just videographers

Most production gets booked one person at a time — a shooter here, an editor somewhere else. We run it in-house end to end, we're backed by Chilli, the marketing agency that's been growing Sunshine Coast businesses since 1993, and we bring our own production vans.

There are two: a 4X4 running solar, lithium, shore power and a 3000W inverter for remote and overnight work, and a 2026 Transit Custom Trail that handles the day-to-day production and grip. A factory floor, a plantation track or a beach at first light works as easily as a boardroom — and they keep the kit in condition, the footage reviewable on the spot, and the coffee coming.
