Common questions

Corporate video, answered.

The questions we're asked most often before a corporate shoot. If yours isn't here, ask us — we'd rather answer it now than after the quote.

How long does a corporate video take from brief to delivery?

accuracy read Most corporate projects run three to four weeks end to end — a week to plan and script, a shoot day, then two weeks in post. Tighter deadlines are usually possible; tell us the date and we'll tell you honestly whether it's on.

What does a corporate video cost?

It depends on shoot days, crew and how much post the piece needs, so we quote per project rather than off a rate card. Every quote itemises what you're paying for, and we'll tell you if something on your wish list isn't worth the money.

Do we need a script before we start?

No. Most clients come to us with a goal rather than a script, and shaping that into a story is part of the job — we're backed by Chilli, a marketing agency that's been doing exactly this since 1993.

Where do you film?

We're based in Birtinya on the Sunshine Coast and shoot across South East Queensland and beyond. Our production vans are fitted out to run a full shoot anywhere, including sites with no power.

Will our staff need to be on camera?

Only if you want them to be. Plenty of corporate work is voiceover, b-roll and on-screen text. When people are on camera we direct them properly — most say afterwards it was easier than they expected.

Can we use the footage across social, not just the website?

Yes, and you should. We build cut-downs for the platforms you're actually posting to as part of the project rather than as an afterthought — one shoot day should feed months of content.

Who owns the finished video?

accuracy read You do. You get the final files to use however you like. We keep the project archived so cut-downs and updates are quick later.

Do you do photography and video on the same day?

Yes — it's usually the most cost-effective way to work, and it's how most of our corporate clients run their shoots. Headshots, team and culture stills and video all come out of one booking.
