What we do

Strategic storytelling and video marketing.

We work with businesses across the Sunshine Coast and beyond to help them cut through the noise and connect with their audience on an emotional level. Whether you need video marketing for social media, YouTube, emails to customers or your website, we make content that earns attention.

When we create videos for your marketing needs, we think about the end game — the real reason you need this video. So whether it's product sales, brand awareness, enquiries or client acquisition, your goal is our main focus throughout the process.

Our backing

Powered by Chilli.

Pepper Productions is a division of the Sunshine Coast's leading marketing agency Chilli, which has been achieving great results for businesses since 1993.

This partnership allows our in-house team of storytellers to get the best results from your content. Be it online, on social media, or for corporate and trade events, we make sure your project is on time, on budget and — most importantly — effective.
