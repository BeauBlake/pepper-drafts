Blurring the Line Between Work and Play

Get a Quote

A Project For the Books

Working with Cure Marine was one of those projects that genuinely blurred the line between work and play.

As a Sunshine Coast video production company, we are always chasing projects that allow us to combine strong visual storytelling with unforgettable locations. Filming aboard the Cure 55 carbon catamaran delivered exactly that.

Our team spent several full days on the water capturing premium video content that reflected the craftsmanship, performance and lifestyle behind the Cure Marine brand. From early starts at Mooloolaba Beach through to long sails up the coastline toward Coolum, every moment was designed to showcase these luxury vessels in their natural environment.

Twin 55's

This project required a balance of creative storytelling and technical precision. Our Sunshine Coast videographers captured cinematic drone footage that showcased the scale and performance of the Cure 55 under sail, paired with lifestyle visuals that reflected the experience of being on board.

Filming along the Sunshine Coast coastline from Mooloolaba to Coolum allowed us to produce high end marine video content that feels both aspirational and authentic. With ideal sailing conditions across multiple shoot days, we were able to deliver smooth, dynamic visuals designed to support Cure Marine’s marketing and sales channels.

All The Little Details

Beyond the lifestyle visuals, we also produced a full walkthrough video with Dave, one of the owners of Cure Marine. This video was designed to give prospective buyers a complete understanding of the Cure 55 carbon catamaran, from design and layout through to the finer details that set it apart.

This style of content is a powerful tool for luxury and marine brands, allowing customers to experience the product before stepping on board.
