Not a nice touch. A different shoot day.

What's in it

Built to work anywhere.

Off-grid power

Solar panels, lithium batteries, shore power and a 3000W inverter. No waiting on a charge halfway through a long shoot.

4X4 capable

A genuine adventure van, so remote locations are a normal shoot day rather than a logistics problem.

Overnight ready

Set up for overnight stays on bigger productions — we start at first light instead of driving to it.

Kit protection

Change room & makeup

Somewhere for talent to get ready on location — the thing most outdoor shoots simply don't have.

The difference

In-house crew. Agency brain.

Most production gets booked one person at a time — a shooter here, an editor somewhere else, and a client left holding it together.

We run the whole thing in-house, from the first planning conversation to the final delivered file. There is no handover between a freelancer and a stranger, because there is no handover — the people who plan your shoot are the people who turn up to it and the people who cut it.

Behind that crew is Chilli, the Sunshine Coast marketing agency that has been growing local businesses since 1993. It changes the first question we ask: not what you want the work to look like, but what it has to achieve.

And the van is the part you can actually see. A factory floor, a plantation access track or a construction site works as easily as a boardroom — no scrambling for power, no half-day lost to logistics.
