Your crew · Meet the tellers

We live to tell your tale.

We're not just a video production company on the Sunshine Coast — we're a team of passionate storytellers who capture the heart of your brand through high-end photography and cinematic video. From first idea to final edit, we offer end-to-end production that's as strategic as it is creative. Whether it's a corporate campaign, a branded tale, or a suite of scroll-stopping visuals, our team is here to help your brand connect.

Beau

Videographer & Editor

Young, dynamic and passionate, with years of videography and editing experience, and a passion for combining new technology with video and image capture. Having shot in Cape Town, through the US, and on Namotu Island in Fiji as their resident photographer, Beau brings loads of global experience.

Jesse

Editor & Shooter

Our in-house editor and on-the-ground shooter — as comfortable behind the timeline as he is behind the lens. With a background rooted in outdoor content and an eye for storytelling, he brings calm energy, curiosity and creative talent to every project.

Trevor

Cinematographer

A cinematographer driven by purpose, drawn to stories with real substance — from ambitious small businesses to conservation projects that make a difference. His process begins with understanding the story, then capturing it through carefully considered shots, with over 30 years' experience behind the camera.

Jack

Hybrid Videographer & Editor

A passion for capturing special moments that traces back to childhood camcorders. Jack's favourite part of Pepper is witnessing the collaborative efforts of the Chilli and Pepper teams as they strategise with clients before, during and after production.
